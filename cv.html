CV

Experimental particle physicist working on neutrino oscillations with T2K, Super-Kamiokande and Hyper-Kamiokande, with a PhD in experimental heavy-ion physics from NA61/SHINE at CERN.

Positions

  1. Sep 2023 – now

    Postdoctoral Researcher (Adiunkt)

    Faculty of Physics, University of Warsaw, Poland

    Experimental neutrino physics in the T2K, Super-Kamiokande and Hyper-Kamiokande collaborations. Research project on neutrino and antineutrino oscillations in Super-Kamiokande, funded by the National Science Centre (NCN), Poland.

  2. Oct 2019 – Apr 2024

    Doctoral researcher

    Jan Kochanowski University, Kielce, Poland · NA61/SHINE Collaboration, CERN

    Experimental heavy-ion physics: search for the critical point of strongly interacting matter in Ar+Sc and Pb+Pb collisions using proton intermittency. Contributed to NA61/SHINE software and to hardware work for open-charm measurements.
